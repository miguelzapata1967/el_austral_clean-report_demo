How the demo works

  1. Click Open the Demo App.
  2. Type a client ID at the top right, one per business you work with: DEMO-001, DEMO-002 and so on. Typing just 1 also works.
  3. Pick a plan, drop the file and click Start clean-up.
  4. When it finishes, click Download all files and save the zip, for example on your Desktop.

Demo limits

  • 100 databases in total for your demo.
  • Client IDs DEMO-001 to DEMO-100.
  • Up to 3 databases per client ID.
  • No charge. Prices are shown so you can see what the service costs.
